JHB Group, New Zealand

Private Investment & Ventures

We invest like it's our own money
Because it is.

JHB Group is a private investment and ventures firm. We commit capital to technology and operating businesses, take an active role, and stay invested for years, not quarters.

New Zealand Scroll

01 About

An investor first, everything else second.

We put capital behind businesses and ideas we believe in, and stay close to them long after the cheque clears.

There are no limited partners to satisfy and no mandate to justify. Decisions get made on their merits, and capital moves when we're convinced, not when a calendar says it should.

The firm's capital sits behind technology, digital businesses and operating companies.

02 Approach

01

BUILD

We develop ideas from the ground up, combining technology, creativity and disciplined execution.

02

INVEST

We allocate capital selectively toward opportunities with compelling long-term potential.

03

DEVELOP

We improve and grow businesses through active ownership, technology and operational discipline.

04

COMPOUND

We think beyond short-term outcomes and focus on creating enduring value over time.

03 Focus

Four areas the firm commits capital to.

01 · Ventures

New businesses, built in-house

We start with a problem worth solving and build the team, product and operating discipline around it from day one.

02 · Technology

Technology-led products

Software and infrastructure where a genuine technical edge is worth paying for, and worth protecting once it exists.

03 · Digital

Digital businesses & IP

Assets built to scale without a matching rise in cost. It's the kind of economics we look for in everything we back.

04 · Operating companies

Strategic positions

Stakes in established businesses where our capital and involvement can genuinely change the outcome.

04 Criteria

What earns the capital.

Not every opportunity gets the same conversation. A few things tend to matter more than the rest.

01

DEMAND

Real customers or users already paying attention, not just a plausible story on a slide.

02

DEFENSIBILITY

Something about the business (technology, relationships, unit economics) that's genuinely hard to copy.

03

HEADROOM

A path to get meaningfully bigger without the underlying economics getting worse.

04

PEOPLE

Founders and operators we'd choose to back again, in a completely different business.

We'd rather be right for ten years than interesting for one.

05 Ownership

Privately held.
Independently minded.

JHB Group is privately owned and answers only to its own long-term judgment. That independence allows for a long view, the freedom to choose its own opportunities, and a focus on outcomes measured over years rather than quarters.
